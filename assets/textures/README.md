# Corn Souls — web textures

- `paintball/<colour>-impact-0<1-5>.png` (blue, lime, orange, pink) — PSX paintball impact decals, 32 px, five shapes per colour — first-party (provided by the project owner). The paintball splats in the TEST channel; the paint colours (in flight and in the gun's magazine) are taken from them. Registered in [../registry.json](../registry.json) as `first-party-psx-paintball-decals`.
- `nibbler/nibbler-NN-<face>.png` (20 faces, 40 px) and `nibbler/nibbler-back.png`, `-rear-quarter-left.png`, `-rear-quarter-right.png` (rear views) — the nibblers, little corn kernels that burst out of dead cob stalkers — first-party (provided by the project owner). Camera-facing sprites in the TEST channel. Registered as `first-party-nibbler-sprites`.
- `fire/fire.gif` (the owner's animated pixel fire, 8 frames at 90 ms, 32 x 24 art at 8x) and `fire/fire-atlas.png` (the same 8 frames at 1x, background transparent, a 256 x 24 strip) — first-party (provided by the project owner). Flames on burning vehicles. Registered as `first-party-pixel-fire`.
- `trees/<NN>-<species>-atlas.png` (10 species, 256 x 128 = 4 x 2 frames of 64 px) and `trees/manifest.json` - PSX scenery tree cards with 8 views each (front, front-right, right, back-right, back, back-left, left, front-left; ground anchor at 32, 62) - first-party (provided by the project owner). **Not used since v1.97.129** - the owner preferred the old code-painted spruce, so the scenery trees are billboards again; the pack stays here, approved and unused, so bringing it back is a renderer change. Registered as `first-party-psx-tree-sprites`.
- `grass/psx-grass-atlas.png` (128 x 64 = six 32 px tufts) and `grass/grass-manifest.json` - PSX grass tufts: short fan, left lean, dense patch, sparse sprigs, tall reeds, wide clump, with the ground anchor at (16, 30) - first-party (provided by the project owner). Scattered over the grass ground only, in streamed 64 m chunks (`TUFTS` in the engine) - never on dirt, asphalt, concrete or floors. Registered as `first-party-psx-grass-tufts`.
- `psx/<name>.png` (16 textures: asphalt, blood_splatter, brick_red, ceiling_tiles, concrete, corrugated_rusty, door_wood, grass, paint_mint, plaster_cracked, sign_exit, steel_rusty, tiles_lino, tiles_white_dirty, wainscot_tiles, wood_floor) and `psx/LICENSE.txt` - **PSX Textures Free by heyheythere - https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-textures-free - CC BY 4.0**. 16-colour dithered PS1 textures, 128 px a metre, unchanged. Ground, concrete, office ceilings and floors, wood doors and the zombie blood splatter use them from v1.97.65 (`PSX` in the engine); the rest are kept for later. Registered as `itch-heyheythere-psx-textures-free`.
