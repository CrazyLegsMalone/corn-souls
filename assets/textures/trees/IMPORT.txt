PSX tree pack

80 individual RGBA PNGs, plus 10 atlases (4 columns by 2 rows).
Frames are 64 x 64 pixels, with a ground anchor at x=32, y=62 from the top left.
Angle order: front, front-right, right, back-right, back, back-left, left, front-left.
Use nearest-neighbor filtering. Select the nearest 45-degree frame to the viewing angle.
manifest.json maps all filenames and atlas rectangles.
